# Character facial artwork

Goku, Naruto and Kick retain their existing facial illustrations and profiles. Boy and Girl retain their existing procedural faces. Those five riders are locked for this refinement pass. Finn, Bart, Shaggy, Light, Peter, Sasuke and Onizuka now use additional character-specific transparent artwork; Spider-Man and Michael Jackson use reference-based native features. `src/player/characters/faces/faceArtwork.ts` preserves the original three-character path and delegates the new decals to `refinedFaceArtwork.ts`. The new pipeline does not change the camera or riding physics.

Artwork originals live in `src/assets/faces/{goku,naruto,kick}.png`. These transparent feature decals were generated with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool. The complete original prompt for each image is recorded in `src/assets/faces/generation.json`; the originals are retained without edits.

The face material preserves the original animated linework and white eyes without environmental lighting washing them out. Transparent pixels expose the modeled skin underneath. Alpha testing removes faint transparent background noise; hair, helmet rims and other opaque meshes still occlude the face normally.

Goku and Naruto use a small display-time expansion of dark ink in `faceArtwork.ts`. It samples four nearby texels, adds coverage around linework, and adapts to a fraction of a screen pixel at gameplay distance. Bright eye interiors and original PNGs remain intact. Kick retains his original face material.

Goku and Naruto also have a close-fitting scalp mesh based on their own head profiles, beneath the larger locks. Its front edge preserves the forehead, with temple sideburns, ear clearance and a lower nape. The extra Naruto crown/rear locks use fewer segments to stay within the existing rider geometry budget.

## Outfit reference notes

The Turtle School 亀 mark is drawn on both of Goku's gi patches, with the front projection reversed in local X so it reads correctly from the front. The reference distinguishes this early gi from the later King Kai and 悟 variants: [Goku's gi symbols](https://dragonball.guru/all-of-gokus-gi-symbols-explained/) and [Turtle School emblem reference](https://www.thedecalmaster.com/Goku-Symbol-Master-Roshi-Kame-Vinyl-Decal-Sticker/).

Naruto's headband has the spiral, pointed leaf base, upward stem and six rivets, using the [Konoha emblem reference](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Simbolo_konoha.svg) and [licensed ABYstyle headband](https://www.estarland.com/product-description/AnimeMerchandise/Naruto-Shippuden-Konoha-Cosplay-Headband/80164). The back patch is a complete round red field with a dark inset whirl, distinct from the headband's leaf stem; compare the [official VIZ jacket back](https://shop.viz.com/products/naruto-bomber-jacket-naruto-uzumaki). These are code-authored contours in `characters/insignia.ts`, with cloth-surface projection rather than external textures.

`preloadCharacterFace` loads only a selected character's local artwork. It resamples the upload to a maximum 512-pixel edge, preserves alpha, and caches the material/texture across mode changes, restarts and ragdoll clones. The startup path awaits the saved rider's artwork. The rider selector marks paused scenes dirty again when a new image finishes loading. A small procedural design remains visible while loading or if an image fails, and failed loads can be retried.

The pause/start menu shows a close-up of the actual selected head on larger screens. `src/ui/riderPortrait.ts` borrows the game renderer for a 192 x 224 capture, shares the head geometry/materials, restores its viewport/scissor state, and then the normal game scene is redrawn. It adds no second WebGL context or rendering loop.

Kick's revised proportions and open-face helmet use [Martin Hsu's Disney production character sheets](https://www.martinhsudesign.com/disney-character-design), alongside the [Disney Channel press reference](https://disneyadvertising.de/app/uploads/2025/05/Presseinformation-Disney-Channel-Kick-Buttowski.pdf). The shell ends at the low brow and sides/back, leaving the chin open; its red stripe follows the same shell. A small omission in the projected face mesh removes the original illustration's nose without editing the PNG. The fallback and head profile also omit the nose.

Kick's anatomy is rendered at 76% scale with an enlarged head and a rounded suit seat attached to the pelvis. Shoe dimensions and deck contact remain full size. Feet and grip targets convert into the smaller rider's coordinates, and hips adjust slightly if a board tweak would exceed leg reach. Crash colliders match the captured anatomy; clones remember that scale when changing outfits. The board's riding collision solver and normal riding trajectory are independent of this visual scale.

## Native character geometry

`src/player/characters/models/cartoonCharacters.ts` builds Finn and Bart. Finn's cloth hood is one connected surface, including the rounded ear roots, around a wide oval face aperture. Dot eyes and a plain closed mouth give him a restrained default expression. His rounded backpack has separate light/dark green halves, an equator seam, clasp, pocket and pull tab; its straps conform to the shirt. The backpack is part of the torso attachment, including in a ragdoll. Scarlet, his original golden sword, is batched into a single cosmetic attached to the right wrist; it follows riding, grabs and the captured crash hand in either mode. The references are the [Finn production turnaround](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/456200637224867316/), [Cartoon Network's Finn evolution video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CbyXlc35Yds), and [the original sword in Season 1](https://www.primevideo.com/detail/Adventure-Time/0NLAYYSJZNK6IC5CL9CPKETEOT).

Bart has his own squared lower jaw and projecting muzzle, continuous serrated crown, white eyes with thin black outlines, a yellow nose and toothy grin. The spikes continue around the back rather than being a front-only silhouette. His build is smaller than Finn's; the latest notes reduce limb length by 10% and increase limb girth by 15%. A character-specific arm-length factor is shared by animated IK and captured crash anatomy. Reference: [Bart turnaround study](https://kenlalli.weebly.com/animation-fundamentals) and [Simpsons production art](https://x.com/SimpsonsArt_/status/1310612472051699714). Finn and Bart have blue shorts over separate bare-leg geometry, exposed knees, white socks and three fingers plus a thumb on each hand. These replace the shared clothed-leg/five-finger meshes on selection, with cached alternatives restored for the other riders.

`src/player/characters/models/humanCharacters.ts` builds Shaggy and Light. Shaggy's longer chin, protruding nose, dot pupils, loose forelocks and individual goatee hairs use the [1977 Hanna-Barbera model sheet](https://scoobymuseum.com/Detail.aspx?Category=12&CollectibleID=5684) and [close-up sheet](https://scoobymuseum.com/Detail.aspx?Category=12&CollectibleID=5696). The green shirt has a projected V-neck; the brown trouser profile widens toward the ankles. Light has a sharper tapered jaw, angular eyelids, brown irises, layered fringe and continuous side/nape hair. His uniform uses white shirt/collar points, notched tan lapels, burgundy striped tie, buttons, pockets and white cuffs, based on the [Death Note production-design collection](https://characterdesignreferences.com/art-of-animation-7/art-of-death-note) and [Light's anime design](https://deathnote.fandom.com/wiki/Light_Yagami).

`src/player/characters/models/peterCharacter.ts` builds Peter's round wire glasses, projecting eyes and nose, short brown scalp/forelock, integrated cleft chin, shirt collar, buttons, pocket, belt and gold buckle. The reference is the [Peter turnaround](https://www.deviantart.com/isquipi/art/PeterGriffinModelSheet-855463481) and [Family Guy production-design collection](https://alextoons.com/blog/2021/12/17/inside-family-guy-pt2). His shirt uses a separate rounded torso profile in `characters/geometry.ts`; the belly's depth and width are modeled rather than added as a floating sphere. Shirt details conform to that profile. His green trousers use cached stocky leg profiles, and his hands have three fingers plus a thumb.

Peter's build supplies wider shoulder/hip roots, pelvis dimensions, shorter legs, and thicker arms. Resting arm targets shift with the shoulder span; board grip and foot targets stay fixed. Sleeve radii fit the thicker arms. His crash colliders cover the wider torso, pelvis, head and limbs. Switching back restores the other riders' cached torso/leg/hand geometry and original limb spacing. Normal board physics and momentum remain independent of these proportions.

`src/player/characters/models/spiderMan.ts` builds the classic red-and-blue suit, with a complete mask, angular white lenses, blue side panels, front/back spider marks, gloves and boots. The mask has no exposed ears, nose or mouth. Thin outward-facing ribbons carry web lines around the mask and limbs, with surface-projected chest webbing and eight-legged emblems. Suit panels and boot shells sit clear of the underlying meshes. Reference: [classic suit front/back](https://www.cosplaycostume.com/us/spider-man-classic-suit-costume-red-blue-classic-spider-man-cosplay).

`src/player/characters/models/animeGuests.ts` builds early-series Sasuke and Onizuka. Sasuke has a distinct pointed jaw, long side bangs, swept rear spikes, a Leaf forehead plate, blue high collar, red/white Uchiha fan, white shorts, wraps and sandals. The collar slopes down at the front so it cannot hide the mouth. His bare-leg geometry is independent of hand anatomy: he retains human fingers. References: [early Sasuke outfit](https://www.nicepng.com/ourpic/u2w7w7u2i1i1u2q8_sasuke-uchiha-naruto-sasuke-wall-scroll/) and [Sasuke production turnaround](https://ekizo.mandarake.co.jp/auction/item/itemInfoEn.html?index=720544). Onizuka has a broad head, strong eyebrows, a toothy grin, a low bleached crop over darker close hair, an open white shirt with rolled sleeves, dark trousers, belt and wristwatch. Reference: [Aniplex GTO key visual](https://www.aniplex.co.jp/lineup/gto/).

`src/player/characters/models/michaelJackson.ts` uses the [official Smooth Criminal video](https://www.michaeljackson.com/watch/smooth-criminal/) for the white fedora/pinstriped suit, blue shirt, white tie and armband. The head has its own jaw/cheek profile, narrow eyes, lips, modeled nose and dark curls. The tilted fedora has an oval pinched crown, closed indented top, dark band, and a thin brim with an underside. Fixed hat transforms are baked into the head batch. Front/back suit pinstripes and lapels follow the torso surface; cuffs and armband inherit the arm motion.

`src/player/characters/primitives.ts` supplies construction helpers for the new guest faces and projected costume details. Joined strokes fill their corners, and ink layers use a greater surface offset than the background patch. The distinct head profiles, eye shapes, brows, mouths, hair and hats remain character-specific. These are stylized first-pass models intended for further likeness refinement.

Hair, clothing and native fallbacks are code-authored geometry with baked vertex colors. Seven new transparent PNGs supply the refined facial features. Each static head/outfit/limb look is batched; hidden costumes do not add draw calls. The existing visible model budgets remain 60 meshes and 20,000 triangles per rider/mode. Anatomy and hand/leg alternatives are defined separately from the board's normal riding physics. Captured crash colliders account for body scale; the rig remains 15 captured parts/14 joints (11 dynamic bodies after combining fixed shoes and hands).

## Refinement artwork and source preservation

`src/assets/faces/{finn,bart,shaggy,light,peter,sasuke,onizuka}.png` preserve built-in imagegen outputs without external image processing. The latest Finn, Bart, Peter and Onizuka images are targeted built-in edits following player feedback. The full active prompts, previous prompts, character/outfit references, generation status and native-only exceptions are recorded in `src/assets/faces/refinement-generation.json`. The image tool declined Michael Jackson (public-figure category) and Spider-Man (output moderation), so those two remain code-authored; their models were still refined from the references. The original Goku/Naruto/Kick PNGs and generation record are preserved.

The new decal pipeline retains a separate colored native fallback mesh per head. Its shared material hides only after a successful image upload, while the shared decal becomes visible. Portrait and ragdoll clones adopt the same transition without extra downloads or scene traversals. Failed loads keep the fallback and can retry. Only the selected PNG is fetched; runtime uploads are limited to 512 pixels, use sRGB, and cache mipmaps across selections and modes. Skin is supplied by the modeled surface, not a pale opaque face fill.

Projection uses measured eye/mouth landmarks from each generated layout rather than assuming identical canvases. Bart and Peter have continuous, smoothly joined eye/nose volumes in their head mesh; this avoids doubled white eyeballs and discontinuous stickers wrapping over separate spheres. Finn's dot eyes and neutral mouth are registered to the wider hood opening. Sasuke's active three-tomoe Sharingan is modeled over the measured iris positions, remaining visible before and after decal loading; his forehead plate and Leaf symbol follow a curved surface close to the band. The older three decal definitions, fallback rasters and ink shaders follow their original path unchanged.

The detailed-notes pass also gives Shaggy a broader long chin and many smaller overlapping hair locks. Light uses a continuous groomed hair shell with subtle strand grooves on the sides/back, retaining his swept fringe. Peter gains a clear lower-lip line, central chin cleft, slightly thicker legs and a small height increase. Spider-Man's hands are 30% larger. Jackson's nose bridge/tip now belong to the continuous head surface; his hair uses connected curl volume with asymmetric forelocks. Onizuka is taller, with a less aggressive brow and more relaxed cocky grin.

Skin tones are matched to the chosen reference appearance: warm peach for Finn/Shaggy/Peter, yellow for Bart, fair warm tones for Light/Sasuke, medium warm brown for Bad-era Jackson and a warmer tan for Onizuka. The same palette is applied to exposed limbs and modeled facial parts. Skin-tone changes do not affect the five locked riders. Temporary review snapshots compare geometry buffers, transforms and materials for all five locked riders in both modes before/after the pass; focused tests cover generated asset packaging, surface projection, lazy loading/failure recovery, shared materials, model budgets and movement contacts.

When adding a face, give it a distinct profile and either native surface-conforming features or transparent artwork. Artwork additions need an asset URL/projection dimensions in `FACE_ARTWORK`. Review front, profile, rear and three-quarter views; keep pupils and mouths in front of the skin, and inspect headgear clearance. Character tests exercise physical invariants, model budgets, visibility, scalp coverage, cached anatomy switching, short-rider contact/grip reach, and crash-clone scaling. Face-art tests check packaged PNGs, UVs, caching, downsampling and offline fallback behavior.

Character builders are dispatched through `src/player/characters/registry.ts`; shared assembly is in `src/player/characters/visuals.ts`. Roster data and persistence live in `catalog.ts`, independently of Three.js, while `skin.ts` applies selection/proportions to live and crashed models. The shared rider rig has no imports of individual character builders. See [the player module guide](../src/player/README.md) for responsibilities and extension steps.

## Triple T (Tung Tung Tung Sahur)

`src/player/characters/models/sahur.ts` uses native geometry for the log silhouette and sculpted facial features, following [the creator representative’s visual-identity reference](https://www.mementumlab.com/wiki-tung-tung) and [the front reference](https://www.pngall.com/brainrot-characters-png/download/381687/). The round trunk overlaps the cylindrical head to conceal the neck during movement. Large warm-white eyes, dark irises, carved brows, a modeled nose and a restrained smile stay visible from the front and side. Vertex colours supply lengthwise grain and growth rings on the closed crown; the character does not fetch an image.

Thin wooden limbs, five-finger hands and bare feet use the existing bone and deck-contact targets. The bat is registered on the right wrist and follows the captured hand during crashes. The registry supplies cached body-part geometry, hidden sleeves/pelvis/default shoes, and fitted log/limb crash shapes. Switching characters restores the previous rig geometry and material flags without modifying shared geometry buffers.

The menu and portrait title use **Triple T**; the catalog and model metadata retain **Tung Tung Tung Sahur**. The face has carved hooded eyelids, large dark irises, a defined nose bridge/nostrils, subtle cheek volumes and sculpted lips. The bat is angled outward from the unchanged wrist grip. A recessed wooden hip connector and shoulder collars close movement gaps without changing joint targets.

## Held weapons

The saved **Show held weapons** display toggle applies to Finn’s sword and Triple T’s bat in both riding modes and in crashes. The prop roots retain character-selection and visibility metadata through cloning. The props remain visual-only, with no weapon collider or melee physics.
